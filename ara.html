How the diagnostic is designed to work
  • Each named surface is checked across five business-approved, equal-weight localities.
  • Two separately initiated checks expose obvious answer variation; a single observation is never treated as stable.
  • Visibility, factual accuracy and enquiry-path completion remain separate measures rather than a blended confidence score.
  • Evidence windows, timestamps, query families and explicit limitations accompany the diagnostic output.

What ARA is—
and what it is not.

ARA is an AIKING offer and working name, delivered through focused pilots rather than a one-size-fits-all platform promise.

How is the agent-ready layer different from AI-search visibility?

The agent-ready layer makes approved business facts easier for machines to interpret. AI-search visibility examines whether and how those facts can be discovered across independent AI experiences. The first builds a reliable source; the second strengthens and measures the signals that lead to it.

Does ARA replace our website or SEO?

No. The structured layer sits alongside the existing human website, and useful content, crawlability, technical structure, listings and conventional SEO remain foundational. ARA makes those foundations clearer for AI discovery and connects them to approved action.

Can ARA guarantee placement in ChatGPT, Google AI or other assistants?

No. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI and other independent systems decide what they retrieve, cite, display and recommend. ARA can improve clarity, access and evidence; it cannot guarantee rankings, citations or recommendations.

Can an ARA agent book, publish or act automatically?

Only where the business has approved the exact action and the connected system supports it. Preparing, recommending, publishing, booking, committing and paying are different levels of authority. Confirmation, approval and human handoff are designed around consequence.

Do we need all three services?

No. A business can begin with the gap that matters most. The strongest sequence usually starts with verified facts, then improves discovery, then adds governed action once the sources and permissions are ready.
