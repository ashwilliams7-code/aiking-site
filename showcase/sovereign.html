CLEAR ANSWERS.

Do I need a website?

No. You can start with two verifiable public surfaces, including at least one owner-controlled profile. Missing sources stay visible rather than being made up.

Will this guarantee I rank first?

No. King AI is designed to help your business be understood and discovered, not to promise a ranking or a sale. Each finding needs inspectable evidence.

Do I have to fill out a huge form?

No. The flow presents one question at a time across Business, Contact and Approval. You review the proposed facts, localities and five questions before consenting.

Can King AI change things without me?

No. Corrections, access and consequential actions have separate owner-approval gates. A diagnostic request is not permission to publish, spend, book or delete.

What can I try in this preview?

The onboarding, scorecard, fact corrections, governed-action preview, billing states, access controls and recovery journey. Everything is browser-only and clearly labelled as a demonstration; no real services are connected.
