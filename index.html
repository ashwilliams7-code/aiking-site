Frequently asked

Questions leaders ask before they move.

Have a specific situation?

Where should a business start with AI?

Start with operating value, not a shopping list of tools. We identify where time, margin, risk or decision quality can improve, then rank those opportunities against feasibility and control.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

Usually not. AIKING is designed around the tools, permissions and rhythms already inside the business. The aim is to add an intelligence layer, not create another disconnected platform.

How do you keep people in control?

Every material action receives a named authority, approval rule and audit trail. The system can prepare, recommend and execute within its boundary; people retain the decisions that matter.

What does a first engagement look like?

Most relationships begin with an executive opportunity review or a clearly bounded implementation. Scope, access, governance and success measures are agreed before any build begins.

Is AIKING only for large enterprises?

No. The fit is determined by the value of the operating problem and leadership’s willingness to implement well — not simply by headcount.
