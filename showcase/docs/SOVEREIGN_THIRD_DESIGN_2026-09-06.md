# King AI — Sovereign / Design 03

An independent, conversion-first third design. It adds a shorter King Kong AU-inspired narrative, subtle photographic motion, one-question onboarding and a five-area visual workspace. Kinetic and Editorial remain preserved rather than overwritten or hybridised.

## Review entry points

- Landing: `sovereign.html`
- Onboarding: `sovereign-start.html`
- Workspace: `sovereign-product.html`
- Neutral three-design launcher: `designs.html`
- Explicit presentation registry: `design-suites.json`

This is an interactive browser-only design prototype. Its evidence, customers, events, prices and states are clearly labelled as examples or internal launch-test hypotheses. It cannot send an email, run a real AI-search scan, authenticate an owner, charge a card, contact a provider, restore cloud data or delete a real account.

## Independent design system

- **Kinetic:** existing photographic command-centre design and twelve-view workspace, preserved byte-for-byte.
- **Editorial:** existing evidence-led dossier design and complete workspace, preserved byte-for-byte.
- **Sovereign:** conversational conversion story; Barlow Condensed and Manrope; rounded photographic windows and visual tiles; a full-screen question stage; five primary work areas with operations on demand.

The live reference `https://kingkong.co/au/` was independently inspected on desktop and mobile. The translated common principles are a sparse header, bold condensed photographic hero, joined input/action, short persuasive letter, visual offer cards, explanatory proof and a recurring clear next step. No King Kong copy, logos, testimonials, customer claims, assets, source code, branded characters or signature graphics are used.

The local font licences and illustrative-photo provenance are in `assets/sovereign/`. Stock photographs are not presented as King AI customers or completed projects.

### What is exercised

- Business → Contact → Approval, one main question at a time; conditional two-profile/no-website path; name/email and declared authority; editable locality/custom-question manifest; explicit consent.
- Facts: proposed claims stay separate; simulated owner/evidence review creates a canonical demo version; unsupported regulated claims remain labelled.
- Discovery: reproducible 400-row synthetic matrix across eight surfaces, five questions, five localities and two runs; unavailable observations excluded, volatility visible, separate numerators/denominators; queued/collecting/validating/released and held-review paths.
- Actions: scope/safety routing, Action-plan capability, verified-owner demonstration, explicit source/approval gates and non-binding human-review queue. Nothing sent.
- Results: direct, assisted, correlated and unknown classes; no blending into invented attributable revenue.
- Operations: billing grace/restriction, named invitation preview, held owner transfer, consent/reconciliation/scope drift/revocation, complete sample recovery ledger, restore preview/undo, pending point/account deletion and undo, retention/offboarding contract and payload-free audit export.
- Motion: working pause/resume on the landing, workspace and desktop onboarding; offscreen suspension; reduced-motion and save-data handling.

### Deliberately not connected

Production authentication, official entity/authority checks, independent fact verification, provider APIs, secure upload/malware scan, Stripe/entitlement reconciliation, hosted data residency, backup/restore infrastructure, email routing, real workflow execution and deletion machinery remain explicit backend integration/release requirements. E5 is not silently selected through the use of fixed example recommendations.

## Run and verify

From the repository root:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8782 --bind 127.0.0.1
python3 showcase/tests/qa_sovereign.py
```

The default runner tests `http://127.0.0.1:8782/showcase`. Set `SOVEREIGN_BASE` and `SOVEREIGN_QA_OUT` to exercise the same suite on another served origin.

A temporary review can use `showcase/tests/serve_design_review.py`. It loads a frozen in-memory allowlist, redirects `/` and `/index.html` to the neutral launcher, and does not serve source tests, Git/config files, private notes or directory listings. It is a review server, not production hosting.

Local regression: **223 checks passed** across 320, 390, 430, 760, 1050, 1280 and 1440 px, full journey interactions and reduced-motion/fontless/save-data fallbacks. The full public interaction gate also passed **216 checks**. The subsequent CSS-only anchor/photographic-tile contrast correction passed the final seven-width colour/geometry check. **53 live files** were byte/hash matched to the frozen snapshot; all tested internal links resolved; six private/source paths returned 404. Both preserved suites were reloaded on phone and desktop with HTTP 200, no page errors and no horizontal overflow. Paired evidence files record the exact scopes.
