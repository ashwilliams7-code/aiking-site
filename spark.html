Where Spark fits

The small engine that gets things done.

Think of Spark as the electric tool on your desk: lean, quick and purpose-built. Give it a repeatable task—sort local files, prepare a daily brief, search approved documents—and it keeps working without becoming a whole cloud platform.

Capability design

Small enough to control.
Strong enough to matter.

Each Spark engagement is built around a concrete outcome, with the operating context and control surface designed before recurring execution begins.

01 / Context

Knows the work.

Approved instructions, reference material, process rules and business definitions are assembled for the job rather than left scattered across prompts.

Context is selected for the workflow, not treated as unlimited access.
02 / Orchestration

Uses what fits.

Models, retrieval, deterministic tools and business integrations are selected against capability, privacy, latency, reliability and cost.

No single provider is assumed to be right for every task.
03 / Control

Stops at the line.

Read, prepare, recommend and act are separate permissions. Escalation, approval and evidence requirements are defined around the consequence of the action.

Named people retain authority over material decisions.
How it works

From friction to a working capability.

  1. Frame the outcome

    Define the business result, current drag, accountable owner and measure that would make the work worthwhile.

  2. Design the boundary

    Map inputs, tools, permissions, data constraints, exception paths and the decisions that remain human.

  3. Build and evaluate

    Implement the workflow, test representative cases and make failures visible before operational use.

  4. Operate and improve

    Monitor quality, cost and exceptions; expand only where observed evidence justifies a wider remit.

Frequently asked

A focused first move.
Built to earn its place.

Spark is scoped for operational learning and measurable value, not novelty.

Is Spark software I buy off the shelf?

No. Spark is an AIKING capability pattern configured around a defined workflow, operating environment and authority model. The resulting implementation depends on the work and systems involved.

How is Spark related to SKY Intelligence?

Sky is the wider personalized intelligence and command architecture. Spark is a focused capability that can operate independently for one workflow or become a specialist capability coordinated within a broader Sky architecture.

Does Spark act autonomously?

Only within the permissions approved for the use case. Gathering, drafting, recommending, triggering and committing an action are distinct levels of authority, with human review used where consequence or uncertainty warrants it.

What makes a workflow suitable?

A useful candidate has a clear owner, repeatable inputs, a visible operating cost or delay, and an outcome that can be evaluated. Highly ambiguous or poorly owned work usually needs clarification before automation.
