05The decision lens

Five questions separate opportunity from noise.

The consultation is not a technology quiz. Ash looks across five connected decisions to find the smallest credible route to value.

01

Strategy

Which outcome matters, how will value be recognised and what deserves to happen first?

02

Agentic systems

What recurring work can be prepared or executed by agents, and where must a person retain authority?

03

Personalised intelligence

Which signals, context and decisions should reach each leader — and in what useful form?

04

Model orchestration

Which models suit the task, cost and risk — and where should multiple capabilities be routed rather than forced through one?

05

Governance

Who can see, approve, override and audit the system before it reaches production?

08Frequently asked

Before you send the note.

If your question is specific to your situation, include it in the briefing. Ash will read it before he replies.

Is the consultation genuinely free?

Yes. There is no fee and no obligation to engage AIKING. It is a focused first conversation to determine whether there is a useful AI decision to make.

How is the time arranged?

Submit the briefing or email Ash directly. Ash replies personally to arrange a suitable time. There is no automated calendar or self-service booking; the time is arranged directly with Ash.

Who will I speak with?

Ash Williams, AIKING’s founder and principal. There is no junior qualification call and no handoff to a separate advisory team.

Do I need an AI strategy already?

No. A recurring bottleneck, decision gap or business outcome is enough. The conversation starts with operating value, not with a preferred tool or model.

Will I receive a complete implementation plan?

No. Thirty minutes is enough to sharpen the opportunity and the next decision, not to replace discovery, architecture or a scoped implementation program.

Should I send sensitive material?

Please do not send credentials, passwords or confidential documents with the first enquiry. If the work progresses, secure channels and information boundaries are agreed during scoping.

Can I email instead of using the form?

Yes. Write directly to ash@aiking.info. The form simply gives Ash the context needed to make the first reply useful.
