03Where it applies

Physical work with a measurable reason.

The first targets are not science-fiction demonstrations. They are tasks where mobility, repetition, safety, labour availability or operating continuity create a real business case.

02

03

Guided assistance, deliveries, room turnover support and repeatable front-of-house tasks.

04

Hazardous or constrained work

Tasks where distance, exposure, fatigue or labour scarcity strengthen the case for physical automation.

Every use case is assessed against the capability of the selected platform, the operating environment and applicable safety requirements before a pilot is proposed.

04Physical authority

When intelligence can move, control becomes physical.

Robotics governance must reach beyond data and approvals. It defines where the machine may go, what it may touch, when it must stop and who can take over.

  • Task and zone permissions

    Named physical areas, tools, objects and actions sit inside explicit operating boundaries.

  • Human supervision

    Manual takeover, emergency stop and escalation routes are part of the operating model—not added later.

  • Testing before exposure

    Simulation, staged trials and acceptance criteria precede real-world expansion.

  • Traceability

    Commands, movement, exceptions, interventions and outcomes return to an auditable record.
