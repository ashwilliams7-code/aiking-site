Frequently asked

Discovery with boundaries.
Action with permission.

ARA is an AIKING product direction scoped through focused pilots, not an off-the-shelf ranking promise.

Does ARA guarantee visibility or ranking in AI assistants?

No. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other independent AI and search systems decide what they retrieve, display and recommend. ARA improves the quality, freshness and actionability of approved business information; it cannot guarantee placement.

Is ARA simply AI SEO?

ARA includes the foundations of AI discoverability, then extends them with source-backed facts, freshness controls and approved customer actions. Its purpose is to make discovery useful, not merely measurable.

Can an assistant complete a booking or purchase?

Only where the business has approved the action and the connected system supports it. ARA can initiate or hand off the next step; confirmations, commitments and payments remain inside the authorised operating flow.

Who controls the data?

The business controls its identifiable data, source connections and published facts. Any aggregate benchmark contribution is optional, de-identified and separately approved.

What makes a good first pilot?

Start with one service line or location where customers ask recurring questions, facts change often enough to matter and one valuable next action has a clear owner.
